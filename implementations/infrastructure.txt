══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  ⚠  DO'S & DON'TS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  ✅ DO's:
  • Keep existing infrastructure services as-is.
  • Dispatch jobs via ::dispatch() for async or ::dispatchSync().
  • Use existing middleware for security (XssSanitization, SecurityHeaders).
  • Use global helpers (gs(), getTrx(), etc.) — they are the standard.

  ❌ DON'Ts:
  • DON'T remove or rename existing infrastructure services.
  • DON'T change job dispatch parameters.
  • DON'T modify broadcast events/channels without checking Pusher/Reverb.
  • DON'T remove global helper functions — hundreds of files depend on them.
  • DON'T change middleware registration order in Kernel.php.
  • DON'T modify the FileManager or ClientInfo libraries.
