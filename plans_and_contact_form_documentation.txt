================================================================================
          BINTEO PLATFORM: MEMBERSHIP TIER SYSTEM & CONTACT MECHANICS
                         COMPREHENSIVE CLIENT GUIDE
================================================================================

This document provides a highly detailed, non-technical handbook outlining:
1. The three membership business lines (Marketplace User Plans, Creator Plans, and OTT Plans).
2. The exact visitor vs. creator subscription relationships required to unlock contact features.
3. The user experience and business rules of the Talent Contact Form.
4. How plan database properties are dynamically rendered to users on the front end.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE THREE INDEPENDENT MEMBERSHIP BUSINESS LINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Binteo features three distinct monetization and access models, each designed for
a specific category of users (Talent, Viewers, and Fans).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. MARKETPLACE USER PLANS (Admin URL: /admin/userplans)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* TARGET AUDIENCE: Creators, Actors, Influencers, and Artists.
* CORE PURPOSE: Enables marketplace talent to upgrade their profiles to gain 
  exposure, acquire search visibility, and receive direct inquiries from industry 
  professionals (e.g. Investors, Producers, casting agents).
* KEY FEATURES & BUSINESS BENEFITS:
  1. Profile Elevation (Featured Placement):
     - Upgraded profiles are placed into high-visibility showcases, such as the
       "Featured Profiles" carousels on the main marketplace pages.
     - They rank higher in directory search results, making it easier for talent 
       scouts to discover them.
  2. Contact and Lead Generation:
     - Unlocks contact options directly on the public profile, enabling casting 
       agents or producers to initiate collaboration.
     - Activates an integrated contact form to receive structured email inquiries 
       (with sender's name, email, phone, and project details).
     - Activates a secure, real-time message chat portal so logged-in users 
       can hold direct messaging sessions back-and-forth.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. CREATOR PLANS (Admin URL: /admin/plans)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* TARGET AUDIENCE: Dedicated Fans and Subscribers of specific creators.
* CORE PURPOSE: A decentralized, creator-centric support model. Individual 
  creators construct customized tiers for their specific channel, allowing fans
  to subscribe directly to them.
* KEY FEATURES & BUSINESS BENEFITS:
  1. Exclusive Video Access:
     - Unlocks premium videos, private playlists, behind-the-scenes content,
       or early-release media uploaded by that specific creator.
  2. Direct Creator Support:
     - A portion of the membership fee supports the targeted creator, enabling
       monetization of their specific follower base.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. OTT PLANS (Admin URL: /admin/ott-plans)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* TARGET AUDIENCE: General Platform Viewers and Entertainment Consumers.
* CORE PURPOSE: The primary platform-wide entertainment subscription model.
* KEY FEATURES & BUSINESS BENEFITS:
  1. Full Catalog Unlock (OTT Access):
     - Unlocks unrestricted access to stream movies, original series, shows,
       and other general media hosted across the entire platform.
     - Operates similarly to mainstream subscription video-on-demand services 
       (like Netflix or Prime Video).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DYNAMIC FRONT-END RENDERING OF PLAN FEATURES & BADGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rather than relying purely on static descriptions entered in the admin panel, the 
front-end interfaces dynamically parse and render visual badges and feature line-items 
based on plan settings.

A. Marketplace User Plans card rendering (Dashboard -> Plans tab):
   - Featured Creator Status (is_featured_plan): If toggled ON, the card renders
     an orange star badge showing *"Featured Profile Placement"* at the top of the features list.
   - Contact Access (contact_access): If toggled ON, the card renders an indigo 
     mail badge showing *"Direct Contact & Live Messaging"* at the top of the features list.

B. Creator Plans card rendering (/client/plans):
   - Featured Tier Status (is_featured_plan): If toggled ON, the card renders a 
     distinct orange badge labeled *"Featured Tier Access"* at the top of the features list.
   - Video Access (video_access): If toggled ON, a green check circle is displayed 
     with *"Premium Content"*. If toggled OFF, a red cross is shown and the text is 
     crossed out.

C. OTT Plans card rendering (/mini-ott and /ott-plans):
   - OTT Access (ott_access): A dedicated row displays *"OTT Media Streaming"*. 
     If toggled ON, it displays with a green checkmark and highlighted text. If toggled OFF,
     it displays with a red cross, muted colors, and a strike-through.
   - Wrapping Logic: Long descriptions wrap cleanly (no truncation with dots) so 
     users on desktop/mobile can easily read the full details of what they are purchasing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE DETAILED TWO-WAY ACCESS LOGIC FOR THE PORTFOLIO CONTACT FORM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When a visitor views a public talent portfolio (for example: /m/demo-actor-2),
the contact system operates on a dual-gate security check (both Creator Plan 
status and Visitor Plan status).

================================================================================
GATE 1: DOES THE CREATOR HAVE THE REQUIRED PLAN? (TAB & BUTTON VISIBILITY)
================================================================================
The visibility of the Contact Tab and the Action Buttons (Message, Contact Now)
on the profile is entirely controlled by the Creator's plan:

1. Creator HAS an Active Marketplace User Plan with "Contact Access" enabled:
   - The Contact Tab will be displayed in the profile's navigation tabs.
   - The "Message" and "Contact Now" buttons will be displayed on the page.
   
2. Creator DOES NOT HAVE an Active Marketplace User Plan with "Contact Access" enabled:
   - The Contact Tab is completely hidden from the page.
   - The "Message" and "Contact Now" buttons are completely hidden from the page.
   - (Result: Nobody can contact this creator or see their details).

================================================================================
GATE 2: DOES THE VISITOR HAVE THE REQUIRED PLAN? (TAB & BUTTON INTERACTION)
================================================================================
If Gate 1 is passed (the creator has enabled contact features on their profile),
who can actually click the buttons and view details is checked:

1. Visitor is NOT Logged In (Guest / Non-registered):
   - The contact details (Email, Phone) display lock icons.
   - Clicking the "Message" button redirects the visitor to the Login page.
   - Clicking the "Contact Now" button triggers a "Premium Feature" alert 
     prompting them to sign in and acquire an eligible plan.

2. Visitor IS Logged In, but DOES NOT have an Active Creator Plan (No Premium Access):
   - The contact details remain locked.
   - Clicking "Message" or "Contact Now" triggers a premium lock alert:
     "You need an active subscription to access direct contact details. Upgrade Plan"
     which offers a link to buy a plan.
   - They cannot submit the contact form.

3. Visitor IS Logged In and HAS an Active Creator Plan (With Premium Access):
   - All contact details (Email, Phone) are visible.
   - The contact form is active and they can send email inquiries immediately.
   - Direct messaging/chat is fully functional.

4. Visitor is the Owner of the Profile (The Talent themselves):
   - The creator always has 100% full preview access to their own profile, 
     meaning they see all tabs and buttons unlocked without needing to buy a plan.
